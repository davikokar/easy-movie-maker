Notes

  1. 1View switch. Storyboard and Timeline show the same sequence and share the selection. Storyboard is the default for new projects.
  2. 2Transition badge. Sits on the incoming clip's left edge and reaches into the gap, so it reads as belonging to that clip and overlapping the previous one. Clicking it opens Transition in.
  3. 3Minimum duration. The duration field shows the minimum from both transitions (spec §5.1). A smaller value is clamped, with the hint shown.
  4. 4Media fit. Default (Fit) follows the project default and changes with it. Choosing Fit or Fill pins the clip.
  5. 5Background jobs. Library badges show per-asset work (proxy, HEIC conversion, intermediate). The status bar shows the total.
  6. 6Missing media. A warning badge on the asset and its clips. The status bar link opens the Relink dialog. Missing clips render as a grey placeholder.
  7. 7Caption editing on the canvas. Click the dashed box to type. The placeholder appears only in the editor, never in export.
  8. 8Audio summary. A read-only overview of music and narration. Timing edits happen in the Timeline. Fit to Music is in the toolbar and the Audio menu.
  9. 9Reorder. Drag cards. A blue insertion bar shows the drop position. Ctrl/Shift multi-select moves a group. Captions and narration follow their anchors.
  10. 10Effect order. Effects apply top to bottom. Drag handles reorder them.

Open questions

  • Should the storyboard show captions only as a CC badge, or not at all?
  • Should the storyboard card width reflect duration, or stay fixed (shown)?
  • Should the library show how often each asset is used (×2) and offer "remove unused"?
  • Preview quality: keep the "Auto / Full / Proxy" switch, or always automatic?
