Notes

  1. 1Pictures lane. One lane. Each incoming clip is drawn over the end of the outgoing clip, and the hatched overlap shows the transition and its duration. Dragging the hatched area's edge changes the duration, within the minimum-duration limits.
  2. 2Transition from the background. The first clip's transition is darker and does not shift timing.
  3. 3Captions lane. Above the pictures, because captions render above them. One caption can span several clips.
  4. 4Anchor link. For the selected caption or narration clip, a dashed link runs from the anchor clip's start to the item, labelled with the offset. The anchor clip gets a dashed outline. Dragging the item re-anchors it to the clip under its new start.
  5. 5Video audio. Embedded audio of video clips, with its crossfade at transitions. It can be muted per lane or disabled per clip.
  6. 6Narration. One lane. The red button records at the playhead, and the recording is anchored to the clip there.
  7. 7Ducking. The orange volume line drops while narration plays, ramping down just before it starts and up after it ends. A gap between narration clips shorter than the ramps stays ducked.
  8. 8Music tracks. Absolute times. Each track has mute and volume. Overlapping songs need a second track ("+ Add music track").
  9. 9Notice with Undo. Automatic adjustments, such as a shortened caption, a shortened transition, or a removed narration clip, show a toast that disappears after a few seconds. Undo reverts the whole command.
  10. 10Diagnostics. Compiler diagnostics, such as an unknown preset or a missing file, are counted in the status bar. Clicking opens the diagnostics panel.
  11. 11Trim and duration. Dragging a clip's right edge trims a video or changes a photo's duration. Later clips ripple, and anchored items follow.

Open questions

  • Show anchor links only for the selection (shown), or for all items, or on hover?
  • Should dragging captions and narration snap to clip starts?
  • Embedded video audio: separate lane (shown), or drawn inside the video clip?
  • Is the per-clip volume line editable directly on the timeline, or only in properties?
